## Honest assessment

- **The launch-board tier is mostly vanity.** Sites 26–50 are small upvote leaderboards
  with near-zero lasting traffic. A Product Hunt day generates a spike and nothing more.
  Useful for a backlink and a badge, not for customers.
- **The review directories are the ones that actually convert.** G2, Capterra, GetApp and
  TrustRadius are where business buyers genuinely search, and profiles there get organic
  traffic for years. They take real effort (verified reviews), which is why they work.
- **AI directories (19–25) are pay-to-play and mostly SEO farms.** Several charge for a
  listing that never ranks. Free submissions (Future Tools, Fazier) are worth the 10 minutes;
  paid ones are usually not.
- **The genuinely high-value items on this list are the free, real-audience ones:**
  Hacker News Show HN, the right subreddit, Indie Hackers, GitHub, and Wellfound.
- **Duplicates and paywalls abound.** Several entries are the same operator's properties or
  are "free but pay to skip the queue" — read the fine print before submitting.
